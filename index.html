Open AI Path

A free, open-source route from high-school math to reading and reproducing AI research. Twelve modules, each lesson paired with free material and something to build. Check lessons off as you go; your progress and notes stay in this browser.

    Make it yours

    The whole curriculum lives in path.json. Edit it to reorder modules, swap resources, or add lessons, and this page updates. MIT licensed, and contributions are welcome on GitHub. Every link points to free material from the original authors.

    How to use it: do the build task before you mark a lesson done. Reading about backprop is not the same as writing it.
